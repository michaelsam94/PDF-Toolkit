## Full description (4000 chars max)
PDF Toolkit is a practical, privacy-first PDF utility for Android. Merge multiple files, split documents by page range, draw and stamp signatures, and turn Markdown notes into polished PDFs — all without uploading anything to the cloud.

**What you can do**
• **Home hub** — Search recent documents, jump to any tool, and import PDFs from your device
• **Merge PDFs** — Queue files, reorder them, and combine into one document
• **Split PDFs** — Pick a source file and extract custom page ranges into separate PDFs
• **Sign PDFs** — Draw signatures on a vector canvas, adjust stroke color and width, save reusable signatures, and stamp them onto selected pages
• **Convert to PDF** — Write or paste Markdown and generate a formatted multi-page PDF locally

**Built for privacy**
Every operation runs on your phone or tablet. PDF Toolkit does not send your documents to external servers for processing. Your files stay under your control.

**Designed for everyday work**
Whether you are combining reports, splitting a contract, signing an agreement, or turning meeting notes into a shareable PDF, PDF Toolkit keeps the workflow simple with a clean Material Design interface and quick-action shortcuts from the home screen.

Download PDF Toolkit and handle common PDF tasks in one offline app.
